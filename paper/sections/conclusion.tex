\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We built a hybrid RAG pipeline that runs entirely on a laptop and asked
whether the dense/sparse balance should be set per query. Fusion helps when
the two retrievers are comparably strong; on identifier-heavy RFC text BM25 is
hard to beat. Our LLM-free QAF model is cheap and readable but does not
reliably beat a fixed weight, behaving mostly like a shrunk constant, although
the oracle headroom is real. Answers from a local 7B generator do not simply
track retrieval quality: hybrids gave the best answers on RFC, dense retrieval
on HotpotQA. Next we plan to compare against LLM-based weighting such as DAT,
add post-retrieval features and in-domain supervision for the weight, and
grow the human-checked RFC question set.
